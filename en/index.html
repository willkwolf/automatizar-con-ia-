Cross-Cutting Security & Quality Protocol

The 5 Dimensions of the Acid Test

Before signing off on any production delivery, the architecture must withstand a battery of adversarial evaluations:

1. Epistemological
Are all claims backed by verifiable primary sources? Zero unchecked hallucinations.
2. Architectural
Does the system meet latency, cost, and throughput boundaries? Respect for Amdahl's Law.
3. Resilience & Failure
What happens during API timeouts or duplicate webhooks? Strict idempotency and DLQs.
4. Governance & HitL
Who owns operational accountability? Least privilege and mandatory human escalation.
5. Anti-Outcome Bias
Acceptance criteria documented prior to testing. Retroactive metric adjustments prohibited.

Clear Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

Direct answers to the common questions raised by COOs and engineering directors before beginning a collaboration.

Who owns the code and intellectual property of the systems built?
Your company retains 100% exclusive ownership of all source code, configurations, and documentation produced. Everything is deployed directly to your own repositories and cloud accounts (AWS, GCP, private VPS, or self-hosted stacks). We enforce zero vendor lock-in and zero proprietary licensing dependencies.
How do you prevent AI hallucinations or unexpected mistakes in critical workflows?
We engineer under strict Human-in-the-Loop (HitL) protocols. Agents handle preparatory tasks (schema validation, data structuring, drafted responses), but any irreversible, client-facing, or financial action requires affirmative human authorization through a low-friction interface.
How does Boutique Digital differ from agencies offering generic no-code automations?
We apply formal software engineering standards: strict type safety, automated regression testing, version control, partial failure isolation, API idempotency, and sandboxed least-privilege execution. We build decoupled, resilient infrastructure that withstands production volume.
How does Boutique Digital collaborate with Casa Matriz Thinking-as-a-Service?
Thinking-as-a-Service acts as our upstream epistemological and research hub. It provides systems complexity frameworks, anti-slop guidelines, adversarial auditing methodologies (The Acid Test), and academic synthesis from Harvard, Stanford, and ACM research, ensuring our technical delivery rests on durable foundations.
What is the recommended first step to evaluate if our agency qualifies?
The ideal starting point is our 2-week Operational Diagnostic Sprint. We examine your current delivery flow, pinpoint the primary constraint, and deliver a comprehensive TO-BE roadmap with technical specifications, giving you complete clarity before committing to major implementations.
